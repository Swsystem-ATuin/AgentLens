# Code of conduct

AgentLens welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, experience,
and choices of tools. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces is expected to follow this code.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the work and explain disagreements with
  specific evidence rather than attacking the person.
- Welcome questions, constructive criticism, and contributions from newcomers.
- Make room for accessibility needs and different communication styles.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people's time. Ask before sharing private
  conversations or identifying information.
- Take responsibility for your contributions, including work assisted by AI.
  Review generated content and respond constructively when someone finds a problem.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are unacceptable. Repeated unwanted contact and retaliation against
someone who reports a concern are also unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns

For conduct in public project conversations, flag the relevant content for
maintainer review using GitHub's reporting controls when available.

For confidential handling by project maintainers, open an
[issue requesting a private contact](https://github.com/Swsystem-ATuin/AgentLens/issues/new?title=Private%20conduct%20contact%20requested).
Include only the request, without incident details, names, screenshots, or other
sensitive information. A maintainer can arrange an appropriate private follow-up;
GitHub issues themselves are public and do not provide private messaging.

You can separately [report abuse privately to GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
GitHub handles those reports under its own policies; that is not a private inbox
for this project's maintainers. For software vulnerabilities, use the
[security reporting process](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review available evidence, protect reporter privacy where possible,
and consider the seriousness, impact, and repetition of the behavior. They may
ask for a change in behavior, remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently exclude someone for serious or repeated violations.
They may act immediately to protect participants.

People affected by a moderation decision may request a review through the same
private-contact process. A maintainer directly involved in a dispute should seek
review from another maintainer where one is available. Retaliation and attempts
to evade restrictions can result in further action.

This code also applies when someone explicitly represents AgentLens outside the
repository. It does not give maintainers authority over unrelated private activity.
